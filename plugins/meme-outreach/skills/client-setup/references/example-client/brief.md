# Northwind Bookkeeping — Client Brief

> Made-up example client.

## 1. What they do
Monthly bookkeeping and payroll for independent restaurants, so owners get clean books and on-time payroll without hiring an in-house accountant.

## 2. Services
- Monthly bookkeeping and month-end close
- Payroll and tip reporting
- Weekly food and labor cost reports
- Multi-location reporting in one format

## 3. Who they cater to
- **Restaurant owners:** owner-operators of 1–5 locations, 15–150 staff, full-service or fast casual. Good-fit signals: opening a new location, hiring a GM, posting about food costs or staffing.
- **Restaurant group operators:** COOs, ops directors and GMs running 3–10 locations, reporting to owners or investors.

## 4. Locations
United States and Canada. Not targeting outside North America.

## 5. Qualified lead
- Qualified: at least one sit-down or fast-casual location with 15+ staff; payroll run in-house today.
- Not a fit: food trucks or pop-ups with no staff; chains with an in-house finance team.

## 6. Outreach
- Channels: LinkedIn DMs, then email
- Sender: Sam, founder
- Voice: casual, short, one filler word ("honestly", "kinda"), light emoji OK
- Never say: "streamline", "solutions", "financial wellness"

## 7. Why they buy
- Opening a second or third location
- The person who did the books quits
- A bad surprise at tax time
- Food or labor costs creeping up with no clear reason

## 8. Pain points (buyer's words → what it causes)
1. "I do the books on Sunday nights" → exhausted owner, mistakes slip in
2. "I don't know my real food cost until it's too late" → margin disappears quietly
3. "Payroll day is chaos" → late or wrong paychecks, unhappy staff
4. "My accountant only shows up at tax time" → surprises in April
5. "Every location reports numbers differently" → can't compare or fix anything

## 9. Proof points (real only)
None. Don't use proof points.

## 10. Objections
- "We already have an accountant" → we do the weekly and monthly work so the accountant only handles taxes
- "It's too expensive" → compare it with the hours the owner spends on books now

## 11. Next step after a reply
Offer a 15-minute catch-up this week or next to compare notes on how other restaurant owners handle month-end.

## 12. Off-limits
- No jokes about food safety, health inspections or staff immigration status
