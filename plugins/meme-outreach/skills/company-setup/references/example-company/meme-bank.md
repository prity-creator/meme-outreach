# Northwind Bookkeeping — Pain-to-Meme Map

> Made-up example company.

Each row walks one pain until it turns funny: pain → consequence → situation → why it's funny → meme → caption → casual question. ★ marks the rows that work best for prospects who went quiet.

## Restaurant owners

### 1. "I do the books on Sunday nights" ★
- **Consequence:** Owner is exhausted and mistakes slip in
- **Situation:** Doing receipts at the bar after closing
- **Why it's funny:** The boss of the whole place is also the night-shift accountant
- **Meme:** Scene — tired chef in a stained apron alone at a closed restaurant's bar at night, chairs stacked, receipts everywhere, laptop open
- **Caption:** "Me doing the books after a 14-hour Saturday."
- **Casual question:** "Who does the books at your place, still you?"

### 2. "I don't know my real food cost until it's too late" ★
- **Consequence:** Margin disappears quietly
- **Situation:** Finding out last quarter's numbers this quarter
- **Why it's funny:** Reading old news about your own business
- **Meme:** Scene — man staring in disbelief at a grocery receipt so long it reaches the floor
- **Caption:** "Finding out my real food cost three months later."
- **Casual question:** "Do you check food cost weekly, or is it more of a vibe?"

### 3. "Payroll day is chaos" ★
- **Consequence:** Late or wrong paychecks, unhappy staff
- **Situation:** Every phone ringing at once on payday
- **Why it's funny:** One person doing five jobs while staff wait
- **Meme:** Scene — woman juggling three ringing phones behind a busy restaurant counter
- **Caption:** "Payroll day at a 40-person restaurant."
- **Casual question:** "Is payroll day as fun at your place as everywhere else?"

### 4. "My accountant only shows up at tax time" ★
- **Consequence:** Surprises in April
- **Situation:** Accountant disappears for 11 months
- **Why it's funny:** A support system that only appears when it's too late
- **Meme:** Scene — man waving from a train window as it pulls away from the platform
- **Caption:** "My accountant after tax season."
- **Casual question:** "How often do you actually hear from your accountant?"

### 5. "Tips make payroll a nightmare"
- **Consequence:** Hours spent reconciling tips by hand
- **Situation:** Counting tip envelopes at 1am
- **Why it's funny:** High-tech kitchen, shoebox-era payroll
- **Meme:** Scene — a bartender at closing time counting a mountain of crumpled tip envelopes by hand next to a shiny new touchscreen register, labeled "New POS" and "Tip math"
- **Caption:** "Every restaurant's tech stack."
- **Casual question:** "Are tips still a by-hand thing at your place?"

### 6. "Opening a new location doubled my admin"
- **Consequence:** Owner stretched across two sites
- **Situation:** Driving between locations with a laptop
- **Why it's funny:** Growth that feels like punishment
- **Meme:** Scene — man sprinting through a parking lot holding a laptop and a stack of folders
- **Caption:** "Me running books for location #2."
- **Casual question:** "How's the second spot treating you so far?"

### 7. "My bookkeeper quit and nobody knows the system"
- **Consequence:** Books fall behind for months
- **Situation:** A login nobody remembers
- **Why it's funny:** The whole business locked behind one person's password
- **Meme:** Scene — person staring at a laptop login screen surrounded by sticky notes with crossed-out passwords
- **Caption:** "Week one after our bookkeeper left."
- **Casual question:** "Who took over the books when that happened?"

### 8. "Labor cost keeps creeping up"
- **Consequence:** Profit drops without a clear reason
- **Situation:** Schedule looks fine, cost doesn't
- **Why it's funny:** Everyone swears nothing changed
- **Meme:** Scene — a restaurant manager forcing a big smile for a staff photo while holding a printout with a red arrow going up
- **Caption:** "Every Monday morning."
- **Casual question:** "Do you track labor weekly or just at month-end?"

### 9. "I can't tell which menu items make money"
- **Consequence:** Popular dishes might be losing money
- **Situation:** Best seller is quietly the worst earner
- **Why it's funny:** Loving the dish that's robbing you
- **Meme:** Scene — a chef lovingly plating a huge, elaborate burger while a calculator on the pass shows a tiny number, labeled "Best seller" and "Margin"
- **Caption:** "Menu planning, every season."
- **Casual question:** "Ever priced out your best seller and gotten a surprise?"

### 10. "Month-end takes two weeks"
- **Consequence:** Decisions made on stale numbers
- **Situation:** Still closing September in mid-October
- **Why it's funny:** The calendar moves faster than the books
- **Meme:** Scene — person at a desk tearing calendar pages while a stack of paperwork grows
- **Caption:** "Closing last month's books, mid-next month."
- **Casual question:** "How long does month-end usually take you guys?"

### 11. "Spreadsheets everywhere"
- **Consequence:** Nobody trusts any one number
- **Situation:** Five versions of the same report
- **Why it's funny:** More files, less truth
- **Meme:** Scene — man with four different wristwatches on one arm, looking confused
- **Caption:** "Checking which spreadsheet to believe today."
- **Casual question:** "Is it one spreadsheet at your place or a whole family of them?"

### 12. "Cash flow surprises"
- **Consequence:** Can't pay a supplier on time
- **Situation:** Big bill lands the same week as payroll
- **Why it's funny:** Every month is a surprise party nobody wanted
- **Meme:** Scene — woman at a café counter holding three long bills fanned out, mouth open in shock
- **Caption:** "Every first of the month."
- **Casual question:** "Does the first of the month sneak up on you too?"

## Restaurant group operators

### 13. "Every location reports numbers differently" ★
- **Consequence:** Can't compare or fix anything
- **Situation:** Five GMs, five spreadsheet formats
- **Why it's funny:** Same company, five languages
- **Meme:** Scene — five people at a table each holding a different-sized pizza box, arguing
- **Caption:** "Our 5 locations sending month-end numbers."
- **Casual question:** "Do all your locations send numbers the same way, or nah?"

### 14. "Investors want reports I can't build fast"
- **Consequence:** Late, messy board updates
- **Situation:** Building the investor deck at midnight
- **Why it's funny:** Running restaurants by day, a finance team by night
- **Meme:** Scene — tired man in a suit at a kitchen table at night with three laptops open
- **Caption:** "Investor update week."
- **Casual question:** "Who builds the numbers for the investor updates?"

### 15. "One location is losing money and I can't see why"
- **Consequence:** Losses hide inside group totals
- **Situation:** Totals look fine, one store is bleeding
- **Why it's funny:** The average is lying to everyone
- **Meme:** Scene — an ops director squinting at five clipboards spread on a bar counter, each with a different number circled, pen in mouth
- **Caption:** "Reading the group P&L."
- **Casual question:** "Can you see each location's P&L, or mostly the total?"

### 16. "GMs hate doing admin"
- **Consequence:** Reports come in late or wrong
- **Situation:** Chasing GMs for numbers every week
- **Why it's funny:** Great at restaurants, allergic to spreadsheets
- **Meme:** Scene — an exhausted GM asleep sitting up in a booth after close, apron still on, while a laptop on the table shows an unfinished report, labeled "Weekly report"
- **Caption:** "Every Monday."
- **Casual question:** "Do your GMs actually enjoy the reporting part?"

### 17. "Adding locations faster than systems"
- **Consequence:** Each new store adds chaos
- **Situation:** Opening #6 on the same setup as #1
- **Why it's funny:** Building a skyscraper on a garden shed
- **Meme:** Scene — a man cutting a ribbon at a new restaurant opening, grinning, while behind him a colleague is buried under falling binders, labeled "Location #6" and "Reporting"
- **Caption:** "Every growing restaurant group."
- **Casual question:** "What's harder right now, opening stores or keeping the numbers straight?"

### 18. "Payroll across states is a mess"
- **Consequence:** Compliance risk, missed filings
- **Situation:** Different rules in every state
- **Why it's funny:** One payroll, five rulebooks
- **Meme:** Scene — person at a desk buried under stacks of paper forms, only one hand holding a coffee visible
- **Caption:** "Payroll for 3 states, one person."
- **Casual question:** "Is payroll the same everywhere for you, or does every state do its own thing?"

### 19. "Buying from suppliers with no visibility"
- **Consequence:** Overpaying for the same items at different stores
- **Situation:** Two stores paying two prices for the same tomatoes
- **Why it's funny:** Same order, different bill
- **Meme:** Scene — two chefs at a market stall each holding up the same box of tomatoes with different price tags, glaring at each other, labeled "Store 2" and "Store 5"
- **Caption:** "Same tomatoes, two different bills."
- **Casual question:** "Do your stores buy from the same suppliers at the same prices?"

### 20. "Audits and lenders want clean books"
- **Consequence:** Delays on loans or deals
- **Situation:** Scrambling for documents when the bank asks
- **Why it's funny:** Clean kitchen, messy books
- **Meme:** Scene — a spotless, gleaming restaurant kitchen with a chef proudly posing, while a back-office desk in the doorway is buried under receipts, labeled "Kitchen" and "Books"
- **Caption:** "Every lender meeting."
- **Casual question:** "How painful was the last time the bank asked for your books?"
