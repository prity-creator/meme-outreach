# Brief Sections

Every company brief has these sections, in this order. For each: what goes in it, and the question to ask when the sources don't cover it. Ask all missing questions in one message.

| # | Section | What goes in it | Question if missing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | What they do | One or two plain sentences: what the company sells and how it works | "In one sentence, what does <company> do?" |
| 2 | Services | Each service or product, in a few words | "What are the main services or products you sell?" |
| 3 | Who they cater to | Buyer types (e.g. agencies vs. in-house brands), job titles, company size, industries, and the signals that someone is a good fit | "Who buys from you? Job titles, company size, industries. Are there different types of buyers?" |
| 4 | Locations | Countries, regions or cities they target, and any they avoid | "Which countries or regions do you target?" |
| 5 | Qualified lead | What makes a lead worth pursuing, and the disqualifiers (too small, wrong industry, no budget) | "What makes someone a qualified lead for you, and who is not a fit?" |
| 6 | Outreach | Channels (LinkedIn, email), who sends, the sender's voice (casual/formal, emojis, filler words), phrases they always or never use | "Where do you reach out, who sends the messages, and how do they write?" |
| 7 | Why they buy | The triggers and motivations behind a purchase (a hire leaves, peak season, a new location, a bad agency experience) | "What usually makes someone buy from you, and why now?" |
| 8 | Pain points | The buyers' biggest problems in their own words, each with what it causes | "What are the 3–5 biggest problems your buyers complain about, and what happens because of them?" |
| 9 | Proof points | Real customers, results and verbatim testimonials that may be mentioned. "None" is fine | "Which real customers, results or testimonials can we mention? Say 'none' if none." |
| 10 | Objections | Common pushback and the honest answer to each | "What do prospects usually push back on, and how do you answer?" |
| 11 | Next step after a reply | What to offer once someone replies (a call, compare notes, a demo), with any booking link | "When a prospect replies, what's the next step you want?" |
| 12 | Off-limits | Topics, jokes, competitors or claims to avoid | "Anything we must never joke about or mention?" |

## Rules

- Use the buyer's own words for pains wherever the sources have them (call notes, replies, reviews).
- A section with nothing reliable says `Unknown` — don't guess. make-memes will work around it.
- Proof points are copied exactly. Testimonials are verbatim with name and title, or left out.
- Keep each section short. The whole brief should fit on two pages.
