# Northwind Bookkeeping — Client Brief

> **Made-up example.** Northwind Bookkeeping, its clients and its people are fictional. This file shows the brief format. Replace it with your own client's real details.

## What they do
Monthly bookkeeping and payroll for independent restaurants, so owners get clean books and on-time payroll without hiring an in-house accountant.

## Buyers
- **Restaurant owners:** 1–5 locations, 15–150 staff, full-service or fast casual. Good fit signals: opening a new location, hiring a manager, posting about food costs or staffing.
- **Restaurant group operators / GMs:** run 3–10 locations, report to owners or investors, need numbers per location every month.

## Pains (in the buyer's words)
1. "I do the books on Sunday nights" → owner is exhausted, mistakes slip in
2. "I don't know my real food cost until it's too late" → margin disappears quietly
3. "Payroll day is chaos" → late or wrong paychecks, unhappy staff
4. "My accountant only shows up at tax time" → surprises in April
5. "Every location reports numbers differently" → can't compare or fix anything

## Not a fit
- Single food trucks or pop-ups with no staff
- Large chains with an in-house finance team

## Proof we can mention (real only)
- None in this example. With a real client, list named clients and verbatim testimonials here.

## Sender and voice
- Sender: Sam, founder
- Voice: casual, short, one filler word, light emoji OK
- Never say: "streamline", "solutions", "financial wellness"

## Next step after a reply
Offer a 15-minute catch-up to compare notes on how other restaurant owners handle month-end.

## Off-limits
- No jokes about food safety, health inspections or staff immigration status.
