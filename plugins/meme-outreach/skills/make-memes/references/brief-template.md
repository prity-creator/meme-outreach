# Client Brief Template

A brief is one markdown file per client, saved at `~/.claude/meme-outreach/clients/<client-slug>.md` (for example `acme.md`). It's the only source of facts about the client. Keep it short: a page or two is plenty.

See `examples/northwind-bookkeeping.md` for a filled-in (made-up) example.

## When there's no brief: questions to ask

Ask these in one message. Accept short or partial answers. If the user gives a website or one-pager, read it first and only ask what's still missing.

1. **Client:** what's the company called, and what do they do in one sentence?
2. **Buyers:** who do they sell to? Job titles, company size, industries. Are there different buyer types (for example agencies vs. in-house brands)?
3. **Pains:** what are the 3–5 biggest problems those buyers have, in the buyers' own words?
4. **Consequences:** what happens because of those problems? (Lost money, late nights, angry clients, missed targets.)
5. **Proof:** which real clients, results or testimonials are we allowed to mention? (Say "none" if none — we'll never make any up.)
6. **Sender:** who sends the messages, and how do they write? (Casual or formal, emojis or not, any phrases they always or never use.)
7. **Next step:** after someone replies, what's the goal? (A call, "compare notes", a demo, a reply.)
8. **Off-limits:** anything we must never joke about, mention or compare to?

Then fill in the template below, show it to the user, and save it once they confirm.

## Template

```markdown
# <Client name> — Client Brief

## What they do
<One or two sentences. What they sell and how it works.>

## Buyers
- **<Buyer type 1>:** <titles, company size, industries, signals that someone is a good fit>
- **<Buyer type 2>:** <...>

## Pains (in the buyer's words)
1. <pain> → <what it causes>
2. <pain> → <what it causes>
3. <pain> → <what it causes>

## Not a fit
- <disqualifiers: too small, wrong industry, already has X>

## Proof we can mention (real only)
- <client name> — <what was done, in plain words>
- <testimonial, verbatim, with name and title>
(If none: "None yet — don't use proof points.")

## Sender and voice
- Sender: <name, role>
- Voice: <e.g. casual, short, one filler word like "honestly", light emoji OK>
- Never say: <words or claims to avoid>

## Next step after a reply
<e.g. "offer to catch up this week or next to compare notes, with one proof point">

## Off-limits
- <topics, jokes or comparisons to avoid>

## Meme bank
Memes that fit this client's buyers. Add to this as memes get made and work.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| <type> | <pain> | <scene> | "<caption>" | "<question>" |
```
